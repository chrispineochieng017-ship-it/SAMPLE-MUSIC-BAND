THE MIGOS

ABOUT

'The Migos' as porpularly known is a Hip-hop group of three talented rappers;namely, Quavo, Offset and Takeoff from the United States of America. The group.Megumi plays a pivotal role throughout Jujutsu Kaisen. His meeting with Yuji sets the main story in motion, and his exceptional potential draws the attention of both allies and dangerous enemies. His family ties to the Zenin clan, his growth as a sorcerer, and his internal struggle between self-sacrifice and living for others make him one of the series' most significant and complex characters.
