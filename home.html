01

Group milestones

Band Name
The Migos

Migos was an influential hip-hop group from Lawrenceville, Georgia, formed by three relatives and longtime collaborators: Quavo, Offset, and Takeoff. Their records helped bring the Atlanta trap sound to a worldwide audience, with tightly synchronized ad-libs, quick triplet rhythms, and memorable hooks. Migos gained broad attention with the 2013 single "Versace." Their 2016 hit "Bad and Boujee," featuring Lil Uzi Vert, reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, and the album Culture received a Grammy nomination for Best Rap Album. The group went on to release Culture II and Culture III, with songs including "T-Shirt," "Stir Fry," and "Walk It Talk It."

02

Quavo

Full name
Quavious Keyate Marshall
Role
Rapper, singer, songwriter
Signature
Melodic hooks, harmonies, and ad-libs
Solo albums
Quavo Huncho (2018), Rocket Power (2023)

Quavo's melodic vocal style often gave Migos songs their sing-along center. He has also continued collaborating with artists outside the group.

03

Offset

Full name
Kiari Kendrell Cephus
Role
Rapper, songwriter
Signature
Forceful delivery and fast cadence shifts
Solo albums
Father of 4 (2019), Set It Off (2023)

Offset's tightly packed verses and changing rhythms brought a sharp contrast to Quavo's melodic approach.

04

Takeoff

Full name
Kirsnick Khari Ball
Years
1994-2022
Signature
Precise timing and intricate rhyme patterns
Solo / duo albums
The Last Rocket (2018), Only Built for Infinity Links (2022) with Quavo

Takeoff's calm delivery and focused lyricism were widely admired. He died on November 1, 2022, at age 28; his artistry remains an important part of Migos' legacy.
